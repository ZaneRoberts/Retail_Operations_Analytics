# KPI Definitions

## Revenue

Sum of Sales.

## Profit

Sum of Profit.

## Profit Margin

Profit / Revenue.

## Orders

Count of distinct Order IDs.

## Customers

Count of distinct Customer IDs.

## Average Order Value

Revenue / Distinct Orders.

## Units Sold

Sum of Quantity.

## Average Discount

Average discount applied across order lines.

## Shipping Days

Ship Date - Order Date.

## Loss-Making Orders

Orders where total order-level profit is below zero.
